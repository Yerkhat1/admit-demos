How the numbers are made — read before trusting them

The three built-in markets are representative patterns for learning, not live data. Each is a deterministic price path (no runtime randomness, so results are reproducible) chosen to show one real behaviour: a steady developed market, an emerging market that runs then crashes ~60% then recovers, and a choppy frontier market with no trend. The rule is real: hold the market while its trailing return over the lookback window is positive, sit in cash (0%) when it is negative, and pay the trading cost each time it switches — that cost is exactly why over-trading a trendless market loses. Max drawdown = worst peak-to-trough fall; volatility = standard deviation of monthly returns, annualized. Wire real historical index series (developed / emerging / frontier) through the CSV box and the rule runs unchanged. No invented figure is ever presented as live data. (Core Law 4.)
