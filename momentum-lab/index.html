Where's the data from? (stylized patterns — click to open)

These are stylized market patterns for learning, not live market data. Each market is a deterministic price path chosen to show one real behaviour: a steady developed market, an emerging market that runs then crashes then recovers, and a choppy frontier market with no trend. Xursandbek can wire in real historical index series (developed / emerging / frontier) later and the same momentum rule runs unchanged. The rule itself is real time-series momentum: hold the market while its trailing return over the lookback window is positive, sit in cash (0% return) when it is negative. No invented "real" figures are presented as live. (Core Law 4.)
