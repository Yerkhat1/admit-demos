The physics & chemistry inside
E = ρ·g·h·η·V
Hydro-electricity — physics. Energy from falling water: density × gravity × dam head × turbine efficiency × the volume released. Release more in winter → more power, but that water never reaches the summer fields.
C = M / V
Salinity — chemistry. Concentration = dissolved-salt mass ÷ water volume. Evaporation and irrigation remove fresh water; the salt stays. As the sea shrinks, C climbs — how the Aral went from ~10 g/L to over 70, and the fish died.
Honest note. Upstream is an educational systems model on realistic, published-order numbers — it shows how the tradeoffs are shaped, it is not a validated forecast of any real year.
